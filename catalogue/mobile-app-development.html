What Shapes the Scope

Every app is different — pricing follows scope, not a fixed menu.

These are the factors that shape a proposal. Tell me which apply and I'll come back with a fixed-scope quote.

Platforms

iOS, Android, and/or web from a single Expo (React Native) codebase.

Authentication

Email/password, phone OTP, social login (Apple/Google), guest mode, role-based accounts.

Payments

Card checkout via regional or global payment gateways, wallet/credit systems, revenue splitting.

Real-time features

Chat, live status updates, and push notifications with deep-link routing.

Admin & operations

Dashboards, approvals, content management, reporting, and broadcast tools.

Backend

Postgres schema design, Row-Level Security, Edge Functions, and scheduled jobs (Supabase).

Localization

Multi-language support with full right-to-left (RTL) layout where needed.

Launch

Managed build pipeline (Expo EAS), over-the-air updates, and app store submission support.

No fixed tiers here on purpose — a booking marketplace and a single-purpose utility app need very different amounts of work. Share your scope and you'll get a clear, fixed-price proposal before anything is built.
