About
I build scalable content systems on one side, and architect flagship courses on the other — then wire the two together.

I'm an AI Product Architect and Technical Content Lead at Educative. I build AI-driven pipelines and agents that generate, personalize, and ship technical learning at scale, and I architect flagship System Design courses end-to-end — grounded in technical rigor and instructional design.

The core of that work is an AI content-generation system I built in n8n, since evolved into a proper internal product. It produces single lessons, multi-lesson sets, and full multi-chapter learning paths, publishing straight to the platform — in active use today, and it became the reference implementation engineering used to build the productized version. Around it: two Agentic Course Designers, 11 interactive learning widgets, and a personalization workflow. Collectively this has generated and updated thousands of blogs and guides, recreated courses of 280+ lessons, and cut a core generation prompt from ~11K to ~1K tokens.

Outside of Educative, I take on select freelance automation projects — the same systems thinking, applied to whatever process is eating your team's time. I map the process first, then automate, so nothing gets built that doesn't need to exist.
