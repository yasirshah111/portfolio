About

Content production, re-engineered as a system.

I'm an AI Products Architect and Technical Content Lead. On one side, I build scalable content systems: AI-driven pipelines and agents that generate, personalize, and ship technical learning at scale. On the other, I architect flagship System Design courses end-to-end — hands-on work grounded in technical rigor and instructional design.

I built an AI content-generation system in n8n (later evolved into a proper product) that produces single lessons, multi-lesson sets, and full multi-chapter paths, and publishes them straight to the platform. It's in active use, generating real content, and became the working reference our engineering team used to build the productized version.

Around it, I built two Agentic Course Designers (autonomous agents that turn a brief into structured courses, guides, and blogs), 11 interactive learning widgets, and a personalization workflow — and I own content generation end-to-end for the marketing & sales domain.

Collectively, this work has generated and updated thousands of blogs and guides, recreated courses of 280+ lessons, and cut a core generation prompt from ~11K to ~1K tokens — about 90% cheaper and more reliable.
