— About
I build scalable content systems on one side, and architect flagship courses on the other — then wire the two together.

I'm an AI Product Architect and Technical Content Lead at Educative. I build AI-driven pipelines and agents that generate, personalize, and ship technical learning at scale — and I architect flagship System Design courses end-to-end, grounded in technical rigor and instructional design.

I built an AI content-generation system in n8n (since evolved into a proper product) that produces single lessons, multi-lesson sets, and full multi-chapter paths, publishing straight to the platform. It's in active use today, and became the working reference our engineering team used to build the productized version.

Around it: two Agentic Course Designers, 11 interactive learning widgets, and a personalization workflow. Collectively this work has generated and updated thousands of blogs and guides, recreated courses of 280+ lessons, and cut a core generation prompt from ~11K to ~1K tokens — about 90% cheaper and more reliable.
